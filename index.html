Hello, I'm
Aymen.

I’m interested in how digital technologies impact people, places, and social life.

As an interdisciplinary researcher, I'm keen to examine how artificial intelligence shapes cultural knowledge, archival practices, and the interpretation and representation of social life. My work brings together qualitative inquiry and computational methods to examine how knowledge is produced, organised, and mediated through digital technologies, with a developing interest in applying computational methods to humanities datasets at scale.

About

I bring experience from both academic research and the design of digital services in banking and financial technology.

My undergraduate training combined communication and design with interdisciplinary approaches to science, technology, society, and research. At postgraduate level, I have developed quantitative and technical skills through financial technology and econometrics.

Professionally, I have worked in UX research, product and service design, and digital transformation. These experiences inform my interest in how technical systems are developed and how their assumptions affect the people they are designed to represent or serve.
